### Overview

Tweaks Delight is a mod for Farmer's Delight that adds quality of life features for your client instance of Minecraft.

### Contributing

Your contributions to this project allow Tweaks Delight to be improved to deliver a better gameplay experience for all players. Feel free to submit your code suggestions, bug reports, texture improvements, or translations.

### Features

This mod adds new utilities that helps players craft recipes easily:

- Simply look at a food item displayed in an item frame to instantly view its crafting or cooking recipe, this works with recipes from other Farmer's Delight add-ons as well.
- Hover your crosshair over a Cooking Pot or Skillet to have a quick look of the ingredients inside and the dish currently being cooked.
- When inside a chest or your inventory, hold Shift over any meal to highlight the exact ingredients required, making it easier to pull resources when all your items are everywhere.
